## The short version

Any **noncommercial** purpose is permitted. Commercial use of any kind requires
a separate written license from the copyright holder.

## Can my church use ChurchBridge for our services?

Yes, free of charge. The license explicitly permits use for religious
observance, and separately permits use by any charitable organization
regardless of how it is funded. Running ChurchBridge for your own congregation
is a permitted purpose.

## What else may I do?

- Read, study, and learn from the source.
- Run it for any noncommercial purpose, including personal study, research,
  testing, and hobby projects.
- Modify it and build new works on it, for permitted purposes.
- Distribute it, including your modified versions, as long as recipients get
  these terms and the required copyright notice.

## What may I not do?

Anything commercial, without a license. That includes:

- selling ChurchBridge, or software built on it;
- charging for installation, hosting, support, or integration of it;
- running it as part of a for-profit business or product;
- offering it to others as a hosted or managed service.

You also may not sublicense or transfer your rights, or strip the copyright and
license notices.

## Our church monetizes its stream / runs a paid conference. Where do we stand?

That sits at the edge of "noncommercial," and the license does not draw a bright
line there. Get in touch and we will sort it out — in practice we are not
looking to charge congregations for worship use.

## Is there a date when this becomes open source?

No. Unlike the Business Source License, PolyForm Noncommercial has no change
date and no automatic conversion. These are the permanent terms for every
release published under them.

## Does this grant me trademark rights?

No. "ChurchBridge" is a trademark of Daniel Haines, and the license grants no
right to use it. If you distribute a modified version, give it your own name
and make clear it is not the official ChurchBridge.

## I want a commercial license.

See the repository README for current contact details. Commercial licensing,
hosted ChurchBridge, deployment support, and integration work are all
available.

## Can I contribute code?

Please open an issue first. Code contributions require a contributor license
agreement assigning copyright to Daniel Haines, so that the project remains
under single ownership. See `CONTRIBUTING.md`.
